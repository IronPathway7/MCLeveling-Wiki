# Server Rules

## Discord & Chat Rules
*   **RULE #1: Advertising IRL Trades:** No trading items/currency for real-life money or other games.
*   **RULE #2: Advertising:** No promoting other streams, servers, or services. Casual mentions are okay; advertising is not.
*   **RULE #3: Begging:** Do not ask staff or players for free items, discounts, or handouts.
*   **RULE #4: Prohibited Content:** No jokes or content involving sexual behavior toward minors.
*   **RULE #5 & #6: DDoS / Dox:** Threatening or successfully DDoSing/Doxing is a permanent ban.
*   **RULE #7: First-Joined Trolling:** No extreme comments (racist, homophobic, etc.) within 2 hours of joining.
*   **RULE #8: Harassment:** No toxic or disrespectful behavior toward others.
*   **RULE #9: Hate Speech:** Zero tolerance for racism, homophobia, transphobia, or sexism.
*   **RULE #10: Inappropriate Content:** No NSFW/Pornographic content in profiles or statuses.
*   **RULE #11: Lying to Staff:** No false info for compensation or to get others in trouble.
*   **RULE #12: Language:** Use English in general chats.
*   **RULE #13: Voice Chat:** No intentional loud/disruptive noises.
*   **RULE #14: Punishment Evasion:** Do not use alt accounts to avoid mutes/bans.
*   **RULE #15: Sensitive Topics:** No religion, politics, or self-harm discussions.
*   **RULE #16: Sexual Harassment:** No unwanted advances or remarks.
*   **RULE #17: Spamming:** No excessive caps, emojis, or ping spam.
*   **RULE #18: Staff Disrespect:** Do not harass staff members.
*   **RULE #19: Impersonation:** Do not pretend to be staff or "mini-mod."
*   **RULE #20: Swearing:** No targeted or excessive swearing at players/bosses.
*   **RULE #21: Suicide Encouragement:** Any direct or indirect encouragement is a ban.

## Game Rules
*   **RULE #1 & #2: Alts & Sharing:** Alt accounts and account sharing are strictly prohibited.
*   **RULE #3: Cheating:** No X-Ray, flying, auto-fishing, auto-clickers, or macros.
*   **RULE #4: Claim Locking:** Do not build claims too close to others to block them.
*   **RULE #5: Duping:** No duplicating items or entities.
*   **RULE #6: Mute Evasion:** Do not use signs/books/mail to talk while muted.
*   **RULE #7: Exploiting:** No abusing known or unknown bugs/glitches.
*   **RULE #9: Griefing:** Do not damage claimed or communal areas.
*   **RULE #10: Signs:** No hate speech on signs or claim names.
*   **RULE #11: Builds:** No inappropriate structures or hate symbols.
*   **RULE #12: IRL Trading:** Same as chat; no real-money trading.
*   **RULE #13: Auction House:** No renaming items to misleading names to scam.
*   **RULE #14: Skins/IGNs:** No sexual or offensive usernames/skins.
*   **RULE #15: Lagging:** No intentionally causing server lag with machines.
*   **RULE #16: Redstone:** No autofishers, flying machines, or unsafe factories.
*   **RULE #18: Scamming:** Drop items at your own risk. If you drop it and someone picks it up, it is their item.
*   **RULE #19: Trolling:** No threatening griefing or refusing to leave claims.
*   **RULE #20: TPA Trapping:** No teleporting players with the intent to kill.
*   **RULE #21: Claims:** No setting homes in/near other claims (Min 200 blocks away).
*   **RULE #22: Casinos:** No gambling allowed in claims or elsewhere.

> **The Common Sense Clause:** Bypassing rules via "grey areas" is still a violation.
