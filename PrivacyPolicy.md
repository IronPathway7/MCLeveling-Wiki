# Privacy Policy for MCLeveling

**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026  
**Official Website:** https://mcleveling.com  

This Privacy Policy explains how **MCLeveling** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the **MCLeveling Bot**, access our website (`mcleveling.com`), or interact with our APIs and services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect only the information necessary to track leveling metrics, manage rank synchronization, and display user profiles and role data on our website and bot commands.

* **Discord Profile & Server Data:**
  * **User Identifiers:** Your public Discord User ID, username, display name, and avatar hash.
  * **Server & Role Information:** Server (Guild) IDs, channel IDs, and the list of roles assigned to your account within servers that use the MCLeveling Bot (used specifically to calculate level tiers and display your roles on `mcleveling.com`).
  * **Activity & Level Metrics:** Experience points (XP), message counts, level progression, leaderboard rankings, and timestamps of activity.
* **Minecraft Account Data (If Linked):**
  * If you link your Minecraft account, we collect your publicly available **Minecraft Username** and **UUID** (Unique Identifier) to link your in-game identity with your Discord/web profile.
* **Technical & Web Analytics Data:**
  * IP address, browser user-agent, session cookies (for web dashboard login), and diagnostic error logs when visiting `mcleveling.com` or making API requests.
* **Payment Information:**
  * If you purchase premium features or tiers, transactions are processed by third-party providers (e.g., Stripe, PayPal). We receive basic transaction receipts (e.g., transaction ID, tier type, billing email). We **never** see or store your credit/debit card numbers or bank account details.

---

### 2. Information We DO NOT Collect
* **Passwords:** We **NEVER** ask for, collect, or store your Discord or Microsoft passwords. All logins use official, secure OAuth2 protocols.
* **Private Message Content:** We **do not** read, log, or store your private Direct Messages (DMs) or the written content of your server messages (messages are only processed ephemerally in memory to calculate XP gains and execute bot commands).

---

### 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your collected data to:
1. Calculate, track, and update user XP, levels, and rank progression.
2. Synchronize and visually display user roles and badges on `mcleveling.com` leaderboards and profile cards.
3. Allow server administrators to configure custom role rewards, multipliers, and dashboard settings.
4. Authenticate your web session when managing server settings via OAuth2.
5. Prevent spam, abuse, unauthorized automation, and API exploitation.

---

### 4. Public Display of Information
By participating in a server that uses the MCLeveling Bot or linking your account, certain information—such as your public username, avatar, level, XP, and assigned server roles—may be viewable by other users on public leaderboards and profile pages at `mcleveling.com`. Server administrators have the ability to configure leaderboard visibility in their dashboard settings.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services
The Service interacts with trusted third-party providers to operate:
* **Discord API:** Used for bot operations, authentication, and role data retrieval. ([Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy))
* **Mojang / Microsoft:** Used for verifying Minecraft UUIDs and avatars. ([Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement))
* **Payment Processors (Stripe / PayPal):** Used for handling secure payments and subscriptions.
* **Hosting & CDN Providers (e.g., Cloudflare, Vercel):** Used to deliver and secure website content and APIs.

---

### 6. Data Retention & EU Privacy Rights (GDPR Compliance)
Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users have specific rights regarding their personal data:
* **Legal Basis:** We process data based on *Legitimate Interest* (providing leveling services and role display) and *Consent / Contractual Necessity* (authorizing OAuth login and bot features).
* **Right to Access:** You may request an export of the data we store linked to your Discord User ID or Minecraft UUID.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You may request the deletion of your leveling history, role cache, and profile from our databases at any time.
* **Revoking Access:** You can revoke MCLeveling's OAuth permissions at any time through your Discord account settings (`Authorized Apps`).

---

### 7. Children's Privacy
MCLeveling is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), consistent with Discord's Minimum Age Requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these ages.
