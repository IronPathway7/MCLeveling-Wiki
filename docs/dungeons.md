# Dungeons

### Requirements
*   **Unlock:** The Boss room unlocks only after a certain number of mobs are killed.
*   **Navigation:** Follow the **particle trail** to find the boss.
*   **End:** When the boss is defeated, the dungeon collapses.

### Rewards
*   **Kill Rewards:** XP and Materials from enemies.
*   **Boss Rewards:** XP, Materials, and Boss Reward Chests.
*   **Crystals:** Mine crystals found in dungeons with a pickaxe for **Crystal Shards** (used for crafting or selling).
*   **Dungeon Chests:** Can be opened with a **Key** of the same rarity (crafted at the Blacksmith's Workbench).
