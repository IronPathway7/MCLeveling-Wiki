# Solo Leveling Aspect

## Rank Evaluation
Your rank is determined by your level:
*   **E rank:** Level 0-24
*   **D rank:** Level 25-49
*   **C rank:** Level 50-99
*   **B rank:** Level 100-149
*   **A rank:** Level 150-199
*   **S rank:** Level 200-299
*   **National rank:** Level 300+

## Soul Points
A unique system to protect players.
*   **Dying < Level 24:** No punishment.
*   **Dying > Level 24:** Consumes 1 Soul Point to save your inventory.
*   **Recovery:** You get 1 Soul Point every 1-2 hours (Max 3).

## The Classes
When you join, you choose 1 of 5 classes:

### Assassin
Fast-paced melee fighter. 
*   **Scales with:** Strength & Dexterity. Sense for Crits.

### Warrior
Tanky melee fighter.
*   **Scales with:** Strength & Vitality.

### Archer
Long-range precision.
*   **Scales with:** Dexterity & Sense.

### Mage
Long-range spellcaster & support.
*   **Scales with:** Intelligence & Sense.

### Healer
Medium-range support and healing.
*   **Scales with:** Intelligence & Sense.
